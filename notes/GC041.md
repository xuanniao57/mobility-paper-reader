# Household activity pattern problem with automated vehicle-enabled intermodal trips

Younghun Bahk; Michael Hyland · Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies · 2024

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2024.104930)

## 作者摘要

• We model daily activity-constrained household travel with private AV (PAV), shared AV (SAV), transit, and intermodal modes. • We focus on capturing numerous realistic types of PAV deadheading and transit-based intermodal trips. • We find that 30-40% of PAV distance stems from deadheading, and 10% of households benefit from AV-transit intermodal options. • We find a monotonically increasing but sublinear relationship between AV travel distance and household activity count. • We find that reducing household PAV count may not reduce PAV distance unless the reduction leads to zero household PAVs. Driverless or fully automated vehicles (AVs) are expected to fundamentally change how individuals and households travel and how vehicles use roadway infrastructure. The first goal of this study is to develop a modeling framework of activity-constrained household travel in a future multi-modal network with private AVs, shared-use AVs, transit, and intermodal AV-transit travel options. The second goal is to analyze the potential impacts of AVs—including intermodal AV-transit travel—on (a) household-level travel behavior, (b) household travel costs, (c) demand for transport modes, including transit, and (d) vehicle kilometers traveled or VKT. To meet the first goal, we propose and formulate the Household Activity Pattern Problem with AV-enabled Intermodal Trips (HAPP-AV-IT) that incorporates AV deadheading and intermodal AV-transit trips. The modeling framework extends prior HAPP-based formulations that model household-level travel decisions as vehicle (and person) routing and scheduling problems, similar to the pickup and delivery problem with time-windows. To meet the second goal, we apply the HAPP-AV-IT to two case studies and conduct many computational experiments. We use synthetic activity location data for synthetic households and a fictitious medium-size network with a road network, transit network, residential locations, activity locations, and parking locations. The computational results illustrate (a) the critical role that household AV ownership plays in terms of household travel decisions, modal demand, and VKT, (b) that with AVs, deadheading accounts for 30–40 % of vehicle operating distances, (c) that around 10 % of households in the study region benefit from AV-based intermodal trips, and (d) that those 10 % of households see 5 % reductions in household travel costs and 25 % reductions in VKT on average in the most transit friendly scenario. This last finding suggests that intermodal AV-transit trips may exist in a driverless vehicle future, and therefore, transit agencies and transportation planners should consider how to serve this market. We also propose and test a simple heuristic algorithm that quickly solves HAPP-AV-IT problem instances.

## 中文译文

本文对具有活动约束的家庭日常出行进行建模，涵盖私人AV（PAV）、共享AV（SAV）、公共交通和多式联运方式，重点捕捉多种现实PAV空驶及基于公共交通的AV多式联运行程。结果发现，30—40%的PAV距离来自空驶，约10%的家庭可从AV—公共交通多式联运中受益；AV出行距离与家庭活动数量之间呈单调递增但次线性关系；减少家庭PAV数量未必减少PAV距离，除非使家庭PAV数量降至零。无人驾驶或完全自动化车辆（AV）预计将从根本上改变个人和家庭的出行方式及车辆对道路基础设施的使用。研究首先构建未来多式联运网络中活动约束家庭出行的建模框架，包含私人AV、共享AV、公共交通和AV—公共交通联运选项；其次分析AV（包括AV—公共交通联运）对家庭出行行为、家庭出行成本、交通方式需求（包括公共交通）及车辆公里数（VKT）的潜在影响。为此，我们提出包含AV空驶和AV—公共交通联运行程的Household Activity Pattern Problem with AV-enabled Intermodal Trips（HAPP-AV-IT）。该框架扩展了既有HAPP公式，将家庭出行决策建模为类似带时间窗取送问题的车辆及人员路径与调度问题。我们在两个案例中应用HAPP-AV-IT并开展大量计算实验，使用合成家庭活动位置数据和虚构中型网络。结果显示，家庭AV拥有量对出行决策、方式需求和VKT至关重要；空驶占车辆运行距离30—40%；约10%的家庭受益于AV多式联运，在最有利公共交通情景下其家庭出行成本平均降低5%、VKT降低25%。这表明无人驾驶未来仍可能存在AV—公共交通联运，交通机构和规划者应考虑服务这一市场。我们还提出并测试了可快速求解HAPP-AV-IT实例的启发式算法。
