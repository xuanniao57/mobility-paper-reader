# Spatiotemporal dynamics of ethnoracial diversity and segregation in Los Angeles County: Insights from mobile phone data

Rongxiang Su; Niall Newsham; Somayeh Dodge · Computers, Environment and Urban Systems · 2024

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2024.102203)

## 作者摘要

Ethnoracial segregation persists as a pressing issue in American cities. Understanding these issues is crucial for promoting social equity and justice, and planning more inclusive cities. Prior research has predominantly emphasized residential ethnoracial diversity but has often overlooked or inadequately addressed ethnoracial diversity and segregation in individuals' daily activities and places they visit, due in part to data limitations. This study leverages a dynamic measure of ethnoracial diversity and dominance at the finest spatial scale, specifically at the Points of Interest (POI) level and various temporal contexts. Using one month of privacy-enhanced mobile phone location data in Los Angeles County, California, this study explores ethnoracial diversity and spatial segregation simultaneously in POI visits in LA County. Our findings confirm that individuals' daily mobility in urban areas enhances ethnoracial mixing at activity locations. Empirical results indicate that the diversity of visitors to a POI is significantly higher than the neighborhood diversity where the same POI is located. A significant positive linear relationship was found between the neighborhood diversity of POIs and the diversity of visitors. About 34 % of the variance in the diversity of visitors to POIs can be explained by the neighborhood diversity of POIs. Our results also suggest significant spatial clusters of isolated/integrated areas regarding ethnoracial mixing in people's daily activity locations. Notably, the Hispanic or Latino population tends to stay in their own communities and experiences a higher level of segregation in their daily activity locations. The findings have significant implications for urban planners and policymakers to design targeted solutions and policies to promote social equity, integration, and equal access to public amenities and opportunities in urban spaces. • Individuals' daily mobility in urban areas enhances racial mixing. • Significant spatial segregation in activity locations among different races. • Diversity of visitors is significantly higher than the neighborhood diversity. • Neighborhood diversity explains 34 % of the variance in the diversity of visitors. • Hispanics experience a higher level of segregation in activity locations.

## 中文译文

族裔种族隔离仍是美国城市面临的紧迫问题。理解这些问题对于促进社会公平正义及规划更具包容性的城市至关重要。既有研究主要强调居住地族裔种族多样性，却常因数据限制而忽视或不足以处理个体日常活动及访问地点中的族裔种族多样性和隔离。本研究利用最细空间尺度（兴趣点POI层面）及多种时间情境下的动态族裔种族多样性和主导性度量，使用加利福尼亚州Los Angeles County一个月的隐私增强手机位置数据，同时考察LA County POI访问中的族裔种族多样性和空间隔离。结果证实，城市居民日常移动增强了活动地点的族裔种族融合。POI访客多样性显著高于POI所在邻里的多样性；POI邻里多样性与访客多样性之间存在显著正线性关系，约34%的POI访客多样性方差可由POI邻里多样性解释。结果还显示，人们日常活动地点的族裔种族融合存在显著空间集群。值得注意的是，Hispanic或Latino人口倾向于留在自身社区，并在日常活动地点经历更高水平隔离。研究结果有助于城市规划者和政策制定者设计有针对性的方案与政策，促进社会公平、融合以及城市公共设施和机会的平等获取。
