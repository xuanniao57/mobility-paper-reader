# Human mobility is well described by closed-form gravity-like models learned automatically from data

Oriol Cabanas-Tirapu; Lluís Danús; Esteban Moro; Marta Sales-Pardo; Roger Guimerà · Nature Communications · 2025

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56495-5)

## 作者摘要

Modeling human mobility is critical to address questions in urban planning, sustainability, public health, and economic development. However, our understanding and ability to model flows between urban areas are still incomplete. At one end of the modeling spectrum we have gravity models, which are easy to interpret but provide modestly accurate predictions of flows. At the other end, we have machine learning models, with tens of features and thousands of parameters, which predict mobility more accurately than gravity models but do not provide clear insights on human behavior. Here, we show that simple machine-learned, closed-form models of mobility can predict mobility flows as accurately as complex machine learning models, and extrapolate better. Moreover, these models are simple and gravity-like, and can be interpreted similarly to standard gravity models. These models work for different datasets and at different scales, suggesting that they may capture the fundamental universal features of human mobility. Modeling human mobility is key for urban planning, sustainability, public health, and economic development. The authors show that simple machine-learned closed-form models are as predictive of mobility flows as complex machine learning models, perform well in extrapolation, work across datasets and scales, and are gravity-like and thus interpretable.

## 中文译文

人类移动建模对于城市规划、可持续发展、公共卫生和经济发展问题至关重要，但我们对城市区域之间流动的理解和建模能力仍不完整。一端是引力模型，易于解释却只能较为适度地准确预测流量；另一端是机器学习模型，具有数十个特征和数千个参数，预测更准确却难以揭示人类行为。本文表明，简单的机器学习闭式移动模型能够像复杂机器学习模型一样准确地预测移动流量，并具有更好的外推表现。此外，这些模型简单且类似引力模型，可用与标准引力模型相似的方式解释。它们适用于不同数据集和不同尺度，表明其可能捕捉到人类移动的基本普适特征。人类移动建模是城市规划、可持续发展、公共卫生和经济发展的关键。作者证明，简单的机器学习闭式模型在移动流预测、外推、跨数据集和跨尺度应用方面均表现良好，并且类似引力模型、具有可解释性。
