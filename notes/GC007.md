# Capturing spatial heterogeneity of population-level human mobility via a prior-guided graph neural network

Zhipeng Wang; Tong Zhang; Jing Li; Peixiao Wang; Yu’ang Zhu · International Journal of Geographical Information Science · 2025

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2025.2493215)

## 作者摘要

Abstract Modeling population-level human mobility has been attracting multidisciplinary research attention due to its profound implications for sustainable urban development. However, previous studies have often neglected the explicit consideration of spatial heterogeneity of travel demand, which limits their abilities to accurately estimate mobility flows. In this study, we introduce a prior-guided, data-driven human mobility model that integrates the position of origins and destinations, spatial travel patterns, and physical models as priors to capture spatial heterogeneity of human mobility. Specifically, we introduce the concept of ‘relative attractiveness’ to emulate the underlying driving force for the formation of spatial heterogeneity in human mobility. To learn the embeddings of ‘relative attractiveness’, we propose a suite of methods that integrate prior knowledge and graph neural networks, mainly including a relative position encoding module to encode the position of different origin-destination (OD) pairs relative to the entire geographical space and a message-passing method inspired by the classical physical models to simulate the mechanisms of mobility flow generation. Finally, a gradient boosting regression tree is trained to generate the mobility flow based on the learned embeddings. Extensive experiments on two real-world datasets have showed our model outperforms state-of-the-art data-driven mobility models in terms of accuracy and generalization.

## 中文译文

人口层面的人类移动建模因其对可持续城市发展的深远影响而受到多学科关注。然而，以往研究常忽视对出行需求空间异质性的明确考虑，限制了移动流的准确估计。本文提出一种先验引导的数据驱动人类移动模型，将起点和目的地位置、空间出行模式及物理模型作为先验进行整合，以捕捉人类移动的空间异质性。具体而言，我们引入‘相对吸引力’概念，模拟人类移动空间异质性形成的潜在驱动力。为学习‘相对吸引力’嵌入，我们提出一组融合先验知识与图神经网络的方法，主要包括相对位置编码模块，用于编码不同起讫点（OD）对相对于整体地理空间的位置；以及受经典物理模型启发的消息传递方法，用于模拟移动流生成机制。最后，训练gradient boosting regression tree，基于学习到的嵌入生成移动流。在两个真实世界数据集上的大量实验表明，本模型在准确性和泛化性方面优于最先进的数据驱动移动模型。
