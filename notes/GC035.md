# Simulating human mobility with a trajectory generation framework based on diffusion model

Chen Chu; Hengcai Zhang; Peixiao Wang; Feng Lu · International Journal of Geographical Information Science · 2024

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2024.2312199)

## 作者摘要

Abstract Most mobility modeling methods are designed to solve specific tasks, leading to questions regarding their deficiency in generalizability. Inspired by the bloom of foundation models, we proposed a Trajectory Generation framework based on the Diffusion Model (TrajGDM) to capture the universal mobility pattern in a trajectory dataset by learning the trajectory generation process. The process is modeled as a step-by-step uncertainty-reducing process, in which a deep learning network with a novel training method is proposed to learn from the process. We compared the proposed trajectory generation method with six baselines on two public trajectory datasets. The results showed that the similarity between the generated and real trajectory movements measured by the Jensen-Shannon Divergence improved significantly on both datasets. Moreover, we applied zero-shot inferences on two basic trajectory tasks: trajectory prediction and trajectory reconstruction. The accuracy improved by a maximum of 25.6% on two tasks. The universal mobility pattern that is suitable for solving multiple trajectory tasks is verified, inferring the strong generalizability of our model. Finally, the study provides insights into artificial intelligence’s understanding of human mobility by exploring the way the model maps the trajectory in the latent space into reality.

## 中文译文

多数移动建模方法针对特定任务设计，引发了对其泛化能力不足的疑问。受基础模型蓬勃发展的启发，我们提出基于扩散模型的轨迹生成框架（TrajGDM），通过学习轨迹生成过程捕捉轨迹数据集中的通用移动模式。该过程被建模为逐步降低不确定性的过程，并提出采用新颖训练方法的深度学习网络从中学习。我们在两个公开轨迹数据集上将该方法与六种基线比较，结果显示，使用Jensen-Shannon散度衡量时，生成轨迹与真实轨迹移动的相似性在两个数据集上均显著提升。进一步在轨迹预测和轨迹重建两项基础任务上进行零样本推理，准确率最高提升25.6%。结果验证了适用于多项轨迹任务的通用移动模式及模型的强泛化能力。
