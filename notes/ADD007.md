# Towards fully automated city operations: Integrating agentic AI with urban digital twins

Haowen Xu; Sisi Zlatanova; Xueping Li; Monica Wachowicz; Michael Batty · Computers, Environment and Urban Systems · 2026

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2026.102449)

## 作者摘要

The convergence of generative AI, the emerging agentic AI paradigm, the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and urban digital twins (UDTs) present transformative potential for automating the management of complex urban systems. In this paper, we address how agentic digital twins can generate knowledge-augmented workflows through multi-step reasoning and autonomously execute them via coordinated AI agents equipped with diverse scientific tools. Such agents are capable of discovering and retrieving relevant data, constructing analytical pipelines, interfacing with domain-specific software, and adapting to dynamic urban conditions and user-defined scenarios through reflective reasoning. Drawing on advances in agentic software systems, both from interactive media and personal assistance, and societal, built and operational applications, we illustrate how this paradigm enables knowledge-driven problem solving and automated orchestration of scientific tools to support real-time city operations. Building on our prior prototype development (the RECOIL project) and recent advances in agentic AI systems for smart city applications, we extend and adapt existing agentic AI design principles to align with the functional pillars and lifecycle requirements of UDTs. Based on this synthesis, we propose a more mature and generalized architectural framework to enable automated city-scale operations, including urban logistics, traffic optimization, building energy management, and disaster response. Finally, we outline key research directions and challenges for deploying agentic AI into UDTs, highlighting a pathway towards scalable, user-focused, and semi-autonomous management of complex interdisciplinary urban systems.
