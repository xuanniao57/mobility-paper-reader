# Distorted insights from human mobility data

Riccardo Gallotti; Davide Maniscalco; Marc Barthelemy; Manlio De Domenico · Communications Physics · 2024

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-024-01909-x)

## 作者摘要

Abstract The description of human mobility is at the core of many fundamental applications ranging from urbanism and transportation to epidemics containment. Data about human movements, once scarce, is now widely available thanks to new sources such as phone call detail records, GPS devices, or Smartphone apps. Nevertheless, it is still common to rely on a single dataset by implicitly assuming that the statistical properties observed are robust regardless of data gathering and processing techniques. Here, we test this assumption on a broad scale by comparing human mobility datasets obtained from 7 different data-sources, tracing 500+ millions individuals in 145 countries. We report wide quantifiable differences in the resulting mobility networks and in the displacement distribution. These variations impact processes taking place on these networks like epidemic spreading. Our results point to the need for disclosing the data processing and, overall, to follow good practices to ensure robust and reproducible results.

## 中文译文

人类移动描述是从城市学、交通运输到疫情遏制等众多基础应用的核心。由于通话详单、GPS设备和智能手机应用等新来源，人类移动数据已从稀缺变得广泛可得。然而，人们仍常依赖单一数据集，隐含假设无论数据收集和处理技术如何，观察到的统计性质都稳健。本文在广泛尺度上检验这一假设，比较来自7种不同数据源的人类移动数据集，追踪145个国家超过5亿人。我们发现所得移动网络和位移分布存在广泛且可量化的差异，这些变化会影响疫情传播等在网络上发生的过程。结果表明，有必要披露数据处理过程，并遵循良好实践以确保结果稳健、可复现。
