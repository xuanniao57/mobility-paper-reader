# Collective flow-evolutionary patterns reveal the mesoscopic structure between snapshots of spatial network

Zhongfu Ma; Di Zhu · International Journal of Geographical Information Science · 2024

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2024.2395953)

## 作者摘要

Abstract Uncovering the collective behavior of flows among locations is critical to understanding the structure within an ever-changing spatial network. When a network evolves, there may exist subgraphs within which the internal flows generally follow a rule: the change rates of the flow weight are either collectively high or low. Classic network measures such as degree, clustering, and betweenness can be used to quantify the process of network evolution by profiling the overall characteristics over time. However, it remains challenging to elucidate how a spatial network is evolving without looking at structures where collective changes emerge. To bridge this gap, we introduce the concept of the Collective Flow-Evolutionary Pattern (CFEP) as a mesoscopic description for spatial network evolution. Four types of patterns with distinct features are defined to clarify the collective behaviors of the flow-evolutionary characteristics. We provide an analytical framework that utilizes flow change rates between two snapshots of the spatial network to detect CFEPs as optimized flow evolution (evo-groups). Synthetic experiments are presented to validate the method. A case study of large-scale individual mobile positioning data is conducted in the Twin Cities Metropolitan Area, Minnesota, US to demonstrate how CFEP can effectively understand the evolution of human mobility networks.

## 中文译文

揭示地点之间流动的集体行为，对于理解不断变化的空间网络结构至关重要。当网络演化时，其中可能存在某些子图，其内部流动总体遵循一定规律：流量权重的变化率整体较高或较低。度、聚类系数和介数等经典网络指标可通过刻画随时间变化的总体特征量化网络演化过程，但若不考察集体变化出现的结构，仍难以阐明空间网络如何演化。为弥补这一缺口，我们提出集体流演化模式（CFEP）概念，作为空间网络演化的介观描述，并定义四种具有不同特征的模式。我们构建分析框架，利用空间网络两个快照之间的流变化率，检测作为优化流演化群组的CFEP。合成实验验证了方法，并以美国明尼苏达州双城都市区大规模个体移动定位数据为案例，展示CFEP理解人类移动网络演化的能力。
