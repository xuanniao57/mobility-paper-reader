# From micro mobility to macro patterns: a vector field-based framework powered by a data-driven flow model for extracting coherent micro-macro traffic dynamics

Hongyu Shi; Heng Qi; Zihan Kan; Xiaogang Guo; Luliang Tang · International Journal of Geographical Information Science · 2026

[来源](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2025.2605321)

## 作者摘要

Abstract The escalating complexity of urban traffic congestion makes single-scale analysis inadequate for supporting effective top-down management strategies. However, existing researches face challenges in representing continuous, interpretable macro-scale patterns without excessively compromising the fidelity of micro-scale movement details. To address this, we proposed a bottom-up vector field-based framework powered by a data-driven flow model to extract coherent macro-scale congestion patterns from micro-scale mobility. We first constructed a network-constrained demand vector field using a path flow model to capture micro-scale mobility. Building on this, we developed congestion exposure fields to characterize the direction and intensity of individual congestion exposure. To inform meaningful spatiotemporal aggregation and improve interpretability, we incorporated irrotationality theory as a scale-aware criterion, identifying scales where micro-scale mobility exhibited stable and coherent structures suitable for macro-scale representation. At these scales, a congestion potential field was derived to reveal system-wide risk dynamics. A case study in Wuhan demonstrates that the proposed framework effectively reduces micro-scale noise and highlights coherent macro-scale patterns, which offer actionable insights into local traffic complexity by serving as real-time warnings for areas prone to congestion or exhibiting increased vulnerability. This work provides an interpretable approach for selecting meaningful aggregation scales, supporting integrative assessments of micro-macro traffic dynamics.

## 中文译文

城市交通拥堵日益加剧的复杂性，使单一尺度分析不足以支持有效的自上而下管理策略。然而，现有研究在表示连续且可解释的宏观尺度模式时，面临难以避免过度牺牲微观尺度移动细节保真度的挑战。为此，我们提出一种由数据驱动流模型支持的自下而上矢量场框架，从微观尺度移动中提取连贯的宏观尺度拥堵模式。首先，我们利用路径流模型构建受网络约束的需求矢量场，以捕捉微观尺度移动。在此基础上，我们构建拥堵暴露场，用于刻画个体拥堵暴露的方向和强度。为实现有意义的时空聚合并提升可解释性，我们将无旋性理论作为尺度感知准则，识别微观尺度移动呈现稳定、连贯结构且适合宏观尺度表示的尺度。在这些尺度上，进一步推导拥堵势场，以揭示系统范围的风险动态。武汉案例研究表明，该框架能够有效降低微观尺度噪声并突出连贯的宏观尺度模式，为当地交通复杂性提供可操作的洞见，并可作为易发生拥堵或脆弱性增加区域的实时预警。本研究为选择有意义的聚合尺度提供了可解释方法，支持对微观—宏观交通动态进行综合评估。
